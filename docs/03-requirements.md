# HatchSide requirements

Thirteen of the fourteen requirements are met on paper by the constructable design (HTS-CAL-001); R11, corrosion resistance, cannot be shown on paper and waits for a TRL 4 coupon test. Four results rest on estimates (R5, R7, R8 and R10's design depth) and are marked so. Every requirement is verified by test at TRL 4 or later.

## Assumptions

- Most routine sewer, tank and digester tasks can be done with grab, rake, sampler and pole tools from the rim.
- Crews carry and use a certified gas detector alongside the kit.
- A commodity two-speed hand winch with an automatic load brake is available in the first region.
- Household biogas digesters have an opening of at least 560 mm and a depth of about 2.5 m below the rim; both are to be measured with the first partner.

## Safety

> **Safety:** R4 and R14 are material-handling requirements for tool heads. Nothing in this document qualifies HatchSide for lifting, lowering or rescuing a person, and the winch must never be used for that. A proof load to 1.5 times the safe working load with a competent person present (CalRig) comes before any use over a real opening. Gas testing, traffic guarding and the no-entry rule apply to every trial.
